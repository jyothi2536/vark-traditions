Our Story

Tradition, Woven with Love

VARK Traditions is a USA-based business born from a love for India's rich textile heritage. We bring Indian families across America carefully curated ethnic wear — from elegant men's kurtas and graceful women's outfits to glamorous cocktail sarees and adorable kids' traditional clothing.

Every piece celebrates artisans, quality fabrics and designs that make you feel proud of your roots at every festival, wedding and celebration.
